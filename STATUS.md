## Scoped offline Bible search — 2026-11-15

- Added canonical Whole Bible, Current chapter, Current book, Old Testament and New Testament filters. Searches remain offline, accept no caller-controlled resource path, cap phrases at 200 characters and results at 200, and stop superseded/closed work between books and chapters.
- Current chapter requires a safe canonical chapter context, scans only that chapter in the active book and reports missing chapters truthfully. Automated corpus coverage and a reversed-predicate mutation pass; live Current chapter UI verification remains open.
- Added Exact phrase and All words matching. All-word matching ignores word order and duplicate terms, requires every whitespace-delimited term, and refuses more than 16 entered words.
- Result verses now highlight all literal matches through a fixed `<mark>` and DOM text nodes only. Ranges are case-insensitive, overlapping ranges merge, regex punctuation is escaped, and the target changes only after the detached fragment is complete.
- Result counts now distinguish exactly-at-limit results from genuine truncation by reading at most one extra match. A focused mutation from `>` to `>=` fails on the exact-limit regression.
- The narrow search pane wraps its controls, focuses the phrase field on open, exposes labeled scope/input controls, and announces progress/counts through a separate polite status region.
- Live Obsidian 1.15.1 checks in ignored `.research/qa/clean-gui-011-final` found John 3:16 in Whole Bible, New Testament and Current book scopes, excluded it from Old Testament, reran on scope changes, and displayed `80+ results (first 80 shown)` only when more than 80 matches existed.
- Full verification passes at 128 tests across nineteen files, strict TypeScript, and the production build. Deliberately reversing the chapter predicate, weakening All words to Any word or treating literal input as regex fails the focused suites. This remains a bounded filter slice, not a claim of boolean, regex, morphological or indexed search.
